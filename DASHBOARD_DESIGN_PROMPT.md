# Treger dashboards design prompt

Use both attached Treger dashboard references together as the visual and information architecture brief. They show three related role dashboards: Admin, Supplier, and Driver. Keep their shared Treger branding, compact left navigation, top utility bar, metric cards, tables, status chips, delivery tracking, and clear hierarchy consistent across the three roles.

Apply one overriding requirement: **dark mode only**. Use a near-black shell and sidebar, charcoal cards, subtle slate borders, bright readable text, and Treger red for the active navigation item, key chart, and primary actions. Use restrained green, blue, and amber status colors. Do not add a light theme or a theme toggle; no dashboard surface should switch to a light palette.

Build three responsive dashboards:

- **Admin:** a greeting and current date; KPI cards for suppliers, drivers, orders, pending orders, in-transit deliveries, completed deliveries, and cancelled orders; a seven-day red order trend; order-status ring chart; recent orders table; quick actions; live delivery map and route list; system notifications.
- **Supplier:** a supplier greeting; KPI cards for products, orders, pending orders, in-transit orders, and completed orders; recent orders table; inventory levels with clear low-stock indicators; supplier notifications and delivery performance.
- **Driver:** a driver greeting; KPI cards for today’s deliveries, completed deliveries, in-transit deliveries, and pending stops; today’s delivery table; dark route map, active delivery details, navigation action, route progress, and driver notifications.

Use a compact top bar with a working dashboard search, notification count, current role/profile, date, and links between the three dashboards. The Admin, Supplier, and Driver sidebars should have role-appropriate navigation, a clear red active state, the Treger logo, and a small reliability/status footer. Keep tables horizontally scrollable on narrow screens, collapse the content grids cleanly, and turn the sidebar into a mobile drawer. Preserve the public Treger product website and use working links between it and the dashboards.

Treat the attached images as visual references for layout and content only. The user’s dark-mode-only instruction takes precedence over the light card surfaces pictured in them.
